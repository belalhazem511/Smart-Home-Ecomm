Product detail fixtures cover only a small explicit subset (three products). Images are icon placeholders; the frontend contains no individual product image or font files. Supplied design references are combined desktop/mobile screenshots, not usable individual product assets. Product photos/hero: `ASSET_PENDING`; font fallback is system stack. Existing source and local preview were checked without browser automation.

## Major gaps and decisions

The preview covers a storefront core, cart/checkout preview, customer account basics, merchant-scoped draft offers and a read-only admin sample. It does not complete the journeys in the brief: no wishlist/compare/seller storefront/public support; product detail and individual imagery are sparse; cart stale-offer recovery and payment outcomes are absent; no customer cancellations, returns, warranty or support; no seller onboarding, catalog proposal, inventory, order operations, shipping, aftersales, finance or support; admin is a limited read-only subset without finance, aftersales, settings, support or audit. Demo fixtures and client-side membership are not live authorization or persistence. Product imagery remains `ASSET_PENDING`; browser/visual checks are `NOT_VERIFIED`.

Business decisions still open include operating country/currency/legal entity, merchant/invoice responsibility, payment methods/provider and COD, carrier/shipping responsibility, commission/promotion funding, payout/reserve rules, cancellation/return/warranty policies, product/document requirements, installation scope, and authentication/guest-checkout contracts. UI states and fixture scenarios must still be built while backend integration is pending.
